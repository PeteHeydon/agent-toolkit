---
name: document-summarise
kind: routine
summary: Compress a document without losing what a reader would act on.
requires_capabilities: [file.read]
conflicts_with: []
schema_version: 1
---

- Lead with what the document is for and what it concludes, not with how it is
  organised.
- Keep every claim a reader might act on: numbers, dates, obligations,
  conditions, and anything that names who must do what.
- Preserve hedging. "May be liable" and "is liable" are different documents.
- Cut restatement, throat-clearing and examples that only illustrate a point you
  have already made.
- Name what the document does *not* cover where a reader would reasonably assume
  it does.
- Say the compression you applied — a summary of a contract is not a contract,
  and the reader should know which they are holding.
